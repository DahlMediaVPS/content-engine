# Format: Routine Collapse (Glassier Skin)

**Length:** 30–60 seconds · **Character:** Nicole · **Structure:** 3 clips

Nicole's signature format: amplify the expensive/exhausting alternative to its extreme, then collapse it to one step. Sells time and sanity first, the serum second.

## Clip structure

### Clip 1 — Amplify hook (0–7s)
The extreme, stated flat with a number: "DNA skin testing: five thousand dollars." / "Women are flying to Seoul for this. Fourteen-hour flight, four figures." Vanity-mirror or bathroom set, morning light, her face (the proof) in frame from second one.

### Clip 2 — Collapse (7–24s)
- The turn: "…Or." (beat) "It's topical now."
- Two collapse lanes — pick ONE per video:
  - **DIY lane (top of funnel):** the free remedy — baking soda + Vaseline tier, real steps, real expected result and timeframe. Builds the trust inventory; no product mention beyond a soft tag.
  - **Product lane:** the routine math, on fingers: "Five steps. Now one. Your whole routine is three." Then the spec stack: 100ml, three-month supply, 22 actives, won't burn your face off — I know that's the first question.
- DIY and product lanes never contradict: the serum is the *time-collapse* of the DIY advice, not its replacement argument.

### Clip 3 — Proof + CTA (24s–end)
- Proof beat: her skin close-up under hard-flash (real-skin realism block, mandatory — airbrushed AI skin kills this format instantly). Before/afters only when we own real assets; `[VERIFY]` otherwise.
- CTA: product lane → product page; DIY lane → free skin guide / comment gate.

## Render notes
`topview-ugc-builder` hero-keyframe + SceneCard format. Hard-flash lighting block from `davemedia` on every skin shot. Specific numbers rendered as text overlays ($5,000 → strikethrough → the collapse).

## QA additions for this format
- Every number in the script is real and sourced, or flagged `[VERIFY]`. Nicole's credibility IS her specificity.
- No "anti-aging"/medical territory — texture, glow, time, money. Compliance gate per `engine/consistency-rules.md`.
- The amplified extreme must be true (Korea injection tourism is real; keep receipts). We debunk exaggerators — we don't get to become one.
