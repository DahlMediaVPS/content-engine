# Format: Debunk-Flip 60

**Length:** 60 seconds · **Characters:** Greg #2 solo, or Dr. Becky + Jesse duo · **Structure:** 4 clips

The full Fladlien build. Use when the debunk deserves a real demonstration arc, the flip has history/mechanism worth telling (castor oil), or the duo dynamic carries it. Runs the complete 6-beat structure from `jasons-copywriting` — this format maps beats to clips.

## Clip structure

### Clip 1 — Visual debunk hook (0–8s)
Same rule as the 30: demonstration in motion from frame one, claim the viewer already knows. The 60 earns its extra hook time with a *reveal* — the hot-water apple wax lift, the thing they can't scroll past mid-reveal.

Duo variant: Jesse brings the hack in ("my feed is nothing but this") and starts the demo; Becky watches with the face of someone who knows how it ends.

### Clip 2 — Verdict + why it spread (8–25s)
- The verdict, then one level deeper than the 30 gives: why this myth spread ("it looks like it's working — that film is real. What they *tell* you it means is the lie").
- This is the enemy beat: "the creators pushing this either don't know or don't care which." Claims, never names.
- Duo variant: Jesse voices the viewer's pushback; Becky dismantles it with mechanism.

### Clip 3 — The flip, with depth (25–48s)
The real remedy with its full story: history where real ("three thousand years and counting"), mechanism in plain words, then the you're-using-it-wrong turn — numbered steps for the *correct* method, expected outcome and timeframe stated.

This clip is why the 60 exists. If a script's clip 3 could fit in 8 seconds, it's a 30.

### Clip 4 — Mirror + gate + bridge (48–60s)
- Mirror beat: name the viewer's situation back to them ("if you're in your forties and your feed's been lying to you for free…").
- Gate or direct CTA into one funnel: free guide → detox method → members group. Real member counts only, `[VERIFY]` until confirmed.

## Render notes
As debunk-flip-30, plus: duo scenes follow the `video-clone-builder` two-speaker scene structure (speaker roles, reaction shots, off-axis framing per the Becky/Jesse character file).

## QA additions for this format
- Every clip-3 step passes the intentional-action rule: action + object + visible result, no vague gestures.
- The 6-beat check from `jasons-copywriting` runs in full — a 60 missing a beat fails QA, no exceptions.
