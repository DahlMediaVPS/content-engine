# Format: Survival Hack

**Length:** 30–60 seconds · **Character:** The Survivalist · **Structure:** 3 clips (single skill) or chaptered series (builds)

Teaches one real skill per video using stuff the viewer already has. The persuasion engine here isn't debunking — it's *demonstrated competence* plus stakes. Every video is one page of the ebook, proven on camera.

## Clip structure — single skill (30s)

### Clip 1 — Stakes hook (0–6s)
Situation first, never gear first: "Everything's wet. You need fire anyway." or "If shit goes south, you don't want to be figuring this out with cold hands." Camera is already outdoors, already in the situation.

### Clip 2 — The teach (6–24s)
- Desired-outcome chaining, spoken out loud: "I want fire. Fire needs three things. Here's where each one's hiding on you right now."
- Numbered steps, each with a visible on-camera result (lint pulled from the sock, spark caught, flame up). The intentional-action rule is absolute here — this audience replays and tests.
- One skill only. Adjacent tips go in the next video, not this one.

### Clip 3 — Payoff + CTA (24–30s)
The working result held on screen (fire burning, water clear, shelter standing), one dry line, then the ebook bridge: "That's one page. The whole guide costs less than the tarp you should already own."

## Series variant — builds (lean-to, water treatment setups)
Chaptered across 3–5 videos, each a complete step with its own payoff ("Part 2: the ridgepole. If you get this wrong, nothing else matters."). Every part re-hooks cold viewers and back-links the series. Series finale carries the hardest ebook CTA.

## Render notes
- Real outdoor sets, overcast/golden-hour light, handheld framing per character file. No studio, no tactical cosplay.
- Hands-and-materials close-ups carry the teach; the macro raking-light block from `davemedia` applies to material close-ups (lint, tinder, knots).

## QA additions for this format
- **The technique must actually work as demonstrated.** Verified before scripting, not after. One fake hack ends the account with this audience.
- Water-safety content: no medical claims about waterborne illness treatment — the guide teaches prevention and treatment of *water*, not people.
- Could the viewer perform the skill from the script alone, with no video? If not, steps are underspecified.
