# Master Plan — The Debunk-Flip Content Machine

## The core insight

People are more likely to believe a negative than a positive. A creator saying "this works" is a salesman. A creator saying "this is bullshit, and here's proof" is a truth-teller. We lead with the negative to buy the credibility we spend on the positive.

## Creating the enemy

The holistic content space already runs on an enemy: *"the industry doesn't want you to know."* Those creators made Big Pharma / Big Food the villain and built massive audiences on it.

We flip the script one level up: **those creators are the new enemy.** They're feeding you cinnamon-on-banana garbage that guarantees nothing. We debunk them by name-of-claim (not name-of-person — see compliance), and the audience that followed them for "truth" follows us for *better* truth. We are fighting for the same audience — this is how we take it.

## The Fladlien mechanic (why debunking sells)

1. **Debunk** — "That hack you saved? Doesn't work. Here's why." → viewer thinks *"yeah, that's what I thought"* → instant agreement, instant trust.
2. **Credibility transfer** — "this guy totally debunked it" → we become the calibrated source.
3. **Bridge** — "But here's what cinnamon (or castor oil, or baking soda) *actually* does…" → we redirect the same ingredient/desire toward something real.
4. **Flip** — the real remedy leads to our free guide → community → product. The skepticism we validated in step 1 is now pointed at everyone *except* us.

Full mechanics: `01-debunk-flip-playbook.md`. Script structure: the `jasons-copywriting` skill (6 beats, locked).

## Amplify-then-collapse pricing

Anchor the expensive alternative at its extreme, then collapse to ours:

- "DNA testing to figure out your skin costs $5,000. Try this first." → baking-soda remedy → Glassier Skin.
- "Women are flying to Korea for injections. It's topical now." → serum.
- "A survival course costs $2,000. The lint in your sock is free." → survival ebook.

The extreme is real and cited where possible; the collapse makes our offer feel like the sane middle.

## Output targets

- **Volume:** 10–20 videos/day minimum, across all characters and accounts.
- **Length:** 30s or 60s depending on format (see `formats/`).
- **Every video** ends in exactly one funnel CTA (see `funnels/`). No orphan content.

## The three funnels

1. **Wellness / Total Detox** — free guide → castor oil detox method ("3,000 years and counting") → free members group → MD Klean. (`funnels/wellness-castor-detox.md`)
2. **Glassier Skin** — 5-steps-into-1 serum, routine-collapse positioning. (`funnels/glassier-skin.md`)
3. **Survival ebook** — $7–$30 water safety / survival guide, standalone character + accounts. (`funnels/survival-ebook.md`)

## Non-negotiables

- Character voices are **locked** in `characters/`. A Greg script that doesn't sound like Greg fails QA.
- Every claim passes the compliance gate (`engine/consistency-rules.md` + the `davemedia` skill's FTC block). We debunk grifters — we cannot afford to *be* one on camera.
- Audience numbers, member counts, and testimonial claims must be real or clearly placeholder-flagged `[VERIFY]` until they are.
