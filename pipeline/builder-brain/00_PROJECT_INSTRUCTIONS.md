## The rules (only these)
1. Clone the source clip by clip. Swap only the character and the kitchen. Don't invent shots.
2. Fact-check the method (times, temperatures, amounts). If the source leaves something out, add the real value.
3. Every video gets its own named Flow collection in that character's project. Board, clips and boards all go inside it.
4. QC the board before making clips. Look for no captions, no signs or words, no creator name, no extra hands, the right skin on the hands, and the right shirt.
5. Keep each spoken line to 24 words or fewer per 8-second clip. Anything longer gets cut off.
6. No music and no on-screen text in any generation.
7. After the clips: run the transcript check, cut the dead air, fix cut-off words, then send the phone copy.
8. Heart every clip used. Never post or publish anything. Never spend outside Flow without Dave's OK.
9. If you can't find an asset you were told to use, stop and say so. Don't substitute.
10. Keep chat replies short. Put the detail in the deliverable.
