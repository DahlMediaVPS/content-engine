# Lessons learned the hard way (keep adding)

- Flow sometimes says numbers wrong, like "one hundred degrees" and "three hundred thirty five". Write numbers as "four hundred degrees" and "one hundred and thirty five", and add "Say every word clearly at a calm pace."
- Lines over about 25 words get cut off. A clip that ends mid-sentence breaks the next clip. Split it into two clips instead.
- Repeats happen ("tapioca starch tapioca starch", "roll it into little balls" twice). Cut the repeat in the edit.
- Speech-to-text invents "to you" at the end when the speech has actually stopped. Check the loudness, not just the words.
- Source boards leak the creator's sign or name (SUSAN), captions and other people's hands. QC every board before making clips.
- Never borrow a board or reference from another dish. That gave Becky cheesecake in a donut video.
- Rapid submits, or generating while the upload patch is still active, trip "unusual activity". Pace one clip about every 90 seconds and reload after uploading.
- Claude in Chrome can drop and time out. If it does, stop, save the state to the build log, and resume when it reconnects.
- The clip that combines the bite with the follow-gate line fails more often. Put the bite after the whole line, or drop it.

- [ ] No dead air over about 0.35 seconds
