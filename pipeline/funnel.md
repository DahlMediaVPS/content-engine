Everything flows to the course or the app. Two kinds of content.

## Reel characters (Greg 1, Greg 2.1, Becky, Steve)
- Recipe and drink videos. The planner pulls the recipe from the transcript: the spoken steps are the recipe. Not every video is a recipe; if it isn't, no recipe card.
- Close: "Comment YES and I'll send you the one thing I add that makes all the difference." (or one of the fixed keywords: GET GUIDE, GET RECIPE, YES).
- The DM (LinkDM card) gives a short talk on that one thing: castor oil, then the link to the castor oil page. A "10 best ways to use castor oil" piece is the lead magnet behind it.

- Claims on the oil stay cosmetic. No cure or treatment claims.

## AI Dave
Growth content only. Every reel ends at the course or app through the Notion skill-download card (keyword, then free skill, plus share and follow first).

## Platforms
- LinkDM works only on Instagram and Facebook. Four accounts now (the four reel characters); 5 or 10 later, and AI Dave would be next.
- YouTube, TikTok and X cannot take a DM. There the close is "link is in the description", and the link is in the description (and a pinned comment where the platform allows it).
- So one description body, two closing lines: comment keyword on Instagram and Facebook, link in the description everywhere else.
