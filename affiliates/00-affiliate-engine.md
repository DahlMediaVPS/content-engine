# The Affiliate Engine

The distribution multiplier: ~80 affiliates who take our reverse-engineered script templates, put **themselves** into the videos (Veo 3 photo-to-video), and post to their own audiences with their own affiliate links. We make the scripts; they make the faces; everybody eats.

## Why this works

- Our owned characters (Greg, Becky, Nicole…) are **our** accounts. Affiliates aren't clones of our characters — they're themselves, running our proven *structures*. 80 different faces saying a proven script beats 80 reposts of the same face.
- Some affiliates have real private audiences. A template with a personalization slot lets them add their own value/angle on top — their audience trusts *them*, and the persuasion spine underneath is still ours.
- Incentives are aligned all the way down: they make money → we make money. The engine's job is to make the "they make money" part as close to paint-by-numbers as possible.

## The honest starting point: 4 of 80 sell

Roughly 4 of 80 affiliates produce sales, with one clear top performer. That's not an affiliate-quality problem — it's a **system** problem. Right now, selling requires skills most of them don't have (hooks, structure, CTA hygiene, consistency). The engine removes the skill requirement:

| Failure mode (the 76) | Engine fix |
|---|---|
| Can't write scripts | They never write — templates are pre-written, slots only |
| Post sporadically | Cadence minimums + a fresh template feed so there's always something to post |
| No CTA / broken CTA | CTA slot is pre-built per funnel; they only paste their link/code |
| Generic content, no audience fit | Value-add slot: one place to inject their angle, everything else locked |
| Make up claims (liability) | Claims are locked copy — slots physically can't touch them (see `compliance.md`) |
| Don't know what works | Template metadata carries proof: source video performance + which affiliates it converted for |

Study the top affiliate first (see `playbook.md`) — whatever they're doing differently gets encoded into template usage notes so it transfers to the other 76.

## The pipeline (system map)

```
Dave's video library (winners, competitors, references)
        │  hand-off: links / files / transcripts
        ▼
INTAKE — reverse-engineer structural DNA          → intake-pipeline.md
  format · beats · dialogue rhythm · visual anchors · CTA logic
        ▼
TEMPLATE — locked spine + personalization slots   → template-spec.md
  QA'd via engine/script-qa-checklist.md, stored in templates/
        ▼
AFFILIATE — self-insertion via Veo 3              → template-spec.md §Veo 3
  selfie → scene keyframes → clips → post with their link
        ▼
FEEDBACK — track template × affiliate conversions → playbook.md §Data loop
  winners get more variants; dead templates get retired
```
