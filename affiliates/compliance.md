# Affiliate Compliance Rails

Eighty people posting about our products with financial incentive is eighty liability surfaces pointed at us. Affiliate claims are legally OUR claims (FTC treats endorsers and the brands behind them as jointly responsible). These rails exist so nobody — them or us — gets burned.

## The three non-negotiables (every post, every affiliate)

1. **Disclose the connection.** Clear and conspicuous, before the fold: `#ad` or "I earn a commission if you buy through my link" in the caption's first line — not buried in tag #14, not only in a profile bio. Comment-gate DMs that contain the link carry the disclosure too.

2. **Claims are locked copy.** Every line about what a product or remedy does ships verbatim from the template spine. Affiliates never write claims — not in the video, not in the caption, not in comment replies. The approved answer to "does it cure X?" in their comments is scripted in the template's usage notes.

3. **Experience framing only for their own additions.** If their value-add beat touches results, it's "what happened to me," with no generalization ("this cured my…" → no; "here's what I noticed after three weeks" → yes, if true for them). Never invent an experience they didn't have — a fabricated testimonial is the one thing this whole engine can't survive.

## What affiliates can never say (hard list, in their onboarding)

- Treats / cures / prevents / heals any disease or condition
- "Doctor recommended" / "clinically proven" unless that exact line is in locked copy with substantiation behind it
- Guarantees of results or income
- Made-up numbers — member counts, stats, "studies show." If it's not in the locked copy, it doesn't exist
- Anything about what the FDA/"the industry" is hiding — we debunk that genre; affiliates don't get to join it under our flag

## Veo 3 / AI-generated video specifics

- The affiliate inserting **themselves** into AI-generated scenes is fine — it's them, endorsing under their own identity, and the experience framing rules still apply to anything they say about results.
- No generating other real people, no fake "customer" characters presented as real customers, no AI-generated before/after imagery presented as photographic proof. Product results imagery comes only from the approved asset pack.
- Follow each platform's AI-content disclosure toggle where it exists (TikTok/YouTube have them; use them).

## Enforcement

- Template QA already guarantees the spine is compliant — enforcement is about the slots.
- Spot-check cadence: every affiliate's first 3 posts reviewed, then random sampling weekly (higher sampling on Tier 2–3, since they have more slot freedom).
- Violation ladder: fix-it notice → template feed pause → removal. One fabricated-claim strike skips straight to the pause.
- Keep the substantiation file: every locked claim in every template maps to its source (product spec, tradition framing, owned test). When a platform or regulator asks, we answer with a document, not a scramble.
