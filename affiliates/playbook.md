# Affiliate Playbook — From 4/80 to 40/80

The training and progression system. Goal: make the median affiliate profitable, not just the naturals. They make money → we make money; this file is the "teach these people how to make money" part, systematized.

## Step zero: decode the top affiliate

Before writing training, extract what the #1 actually does differently. Audit their last 20 posts against the converting-3 and against 5 non-sellers:

- Cadence (posts/week) · hook discipline (do they keep our hooks verbatim?)
- Personalization (what do they put in the value-add beat?)
- CTA hygiene (link placement, comment-gate follow-through, DM replies?)
- Audience relationship (do they reply to comments? go live? have an existing niche?)

Whatever separates them becomes **usage notes on every template** and the checklist below. The playbook is reverse-engineered from the winner, not invented — same method as the content itself.

## The tier ladder

| Tier | Who | What they run | What unlocks it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — Poster** | Default, all 80 | Templates as-is: fill slots, render, post | Signing up |
| **2 — Operator** | Consistent posters | Full value-add slot + early access to new templates | 4 weeks at cadence minimum |
| **3 — Partner** | The converting few | Custom scripts in THEIR voice (they become a semi-character with their own bible file) | Attributed sales, sustained |

Design intent: Tier 1 requires zero skill and zero writing. The ladder rewards the two behaviors that actually produce sales — consistency first, personalization second.

## Cadence minimums (Tier 1 rules)

- **3 posts/week minimum, 1/day target.** Below 3/week the algorithm never learns their account; nothing else in this playbook matters.
- One funnel per account. An affiliate posting detox + skin + survival reads as a spam account to both the algorithm and the audience.
- Post the template within its freshness window (debunk templates decay as claims fade — META carries the hook's status from `hooks/backlog.md`).

## The weekly loop (what training actually teaches)

1. Pick 3+ templates from the feed (new + proven-for-others)
2. Fill slots per `template-spec.md` — the one-hour workflow
3. Post, with disclosure (`compliance.md`) and working `{{CTA-LINK}}`
4. **Work the comments** — the top-affiliate behavior most likely doing hidden work: reply, run the comment gates, answer DMs with the link
5. Report numbers (or connect tracking) — feeds the data loop

## The data loop

Track **template × affiliate → clicks/sales** (even manually at first: a shared sheet beats nothing).

- Template converts across many affiliates → make variants, front of the feed
- Template converts for nobody → retire it; the source video's magic didn't survive extraction
- Template converts for ONE affiliate → study *the affiliate*, not the template — that's a Tier-3 candidate showing you their edge
- Affiliate posts at cadence for 4+ weeks with zero conversions → their audience doesn't match the funnel; switch their funnel before concluding they can't sell

## What we owe them vs. what they owe us

**We provide:** a steady template feed, the Veo 3 workflow docs, compliant copy they can't get sued over, conversion data on what's working, and tier progression that's earned, not vibes.

**They provide:** cadence, slot discipline (no editing locked lines), disclosure on every post, and honest numbers.

An affiliate who follows the loop for 8 weeks and still converts nothing gets a funnel switch, then a template-mix change, then an honest conversation — in that order. The system takes the blame before the person does; that's how you keep 80 people posting.
