Questions

Everything you'd ask a K-beauty expert.

What exactly is PDRN / Salmon DNA?
PDRN (polydeoxyribonucleotide) is a purified fragment derived from salmon DNA. In skincare it's prized for supporting the skin's own barrier-repair and hydration processes — a mainstay of Korean clinical facials, now in a serum you use at home.
Is it safe for sensitive, breakout-prone skin?
The formula is fragrance-free and deliberately short — designed for barriers that are already irritated. As with any new active, patch test first, and introduce the derma stamp gradually.
How long until I see results?
Many people notice more hydration and less tightness within the first week or two. Barrier repair is cumulative — consistent nightly use over 4–8 weeks is where the "glass skin" look tends to show.
How do I use the derma stamp?
On clean, dry skin, gently press the stamp across target areas before applying the serum. Keep it sanitised, start 1–2 nights a week, and never share it. The Best Value kit includes it.
