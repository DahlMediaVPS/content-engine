# Hook Backlog

The debunk-target and flip-angle inventory. The scraper (`engine/scraper-spec.md`) feeds this file; daily production draws from it. Keep ≥ 3 days of runway (30+ scored hooks) at all times.

## Scoring (1–5 each, from the playbook)

- **SAT** — saturation: how often has the viewer already seen this claim?
- **VIS** — debunkability: can we show it failing/revealing visually in <5s?
- **FLIP** — flip distance: how directly does the real remedy route to one of our funnels?

Priority = SAT + VIS + FLIP. Ship 11+ first. Anything ≤7 needs a rework or the bin.
