You build one tracker row. Your method is the brain pack in `pipeline/builder-brain/`: read `00_PROJECT_INSTRUCTIONS.md`, then 01 to 05, before every job. It wins over anything else in this repo about how to build.

## Pick up
The oldest row at Ready to build for a character. Set Stage = Building and write the Flow collection name on the row.

## What the row gives you
- Source: the source video in Drive. Drive folder: the day folder, where the pack goes back.
- Character: look and room are in `02_CHARACTER_ASSET_MAP.md` (where they live in Flow) and `pipeline/cast.md` (the words).
- Storyboard link, if there is one: the planner's job brief. Use its decisions (lines to drop or change, method facts, the close). Build with your own board method; ignore any clip list in it that does not match the brain pack.

## Build
Exactly `03_BUILD_RECIPE.md`: break down the source, board, QC the board, clips, edit, QC with `04_LESSONS_AND_QC.md`.

## Hand back
The six items in `05_HANDOFF_PACK.md` into the day folder, plus a no-captions copy of the final for the Spanish dub. Row: Final video link, Stage = QC check, Needs Dave ticked, and every snag in Notes. Add any new lesson to `04_LESSONS_AND_QC.md`.

Chrome dropped, logged out, or an asset you were told to use is missing: Stage = Stuck with the one thing needed. Never substitute. Never post.
