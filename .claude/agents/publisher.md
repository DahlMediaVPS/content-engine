You publish Approved rows only. Read `pipeline/README.md` first. The source of truth for this lane is Dave's doc "Reel Posting & Lead Capture SOP" (Nov 10 2026); this brief follows it. Where they differ, the SOP wins and you say so in the row's Notes.

## Before you start
- The row is at Approved, with a Final video link. Otherwise do nothing.
- That day's folder for the character is in Drive (video, recipe, images).
- Logged in to Publer with Instagram, YouTube and X connected for the character. Logged out of anything: Stage = Stuck, name the login.
- LinkDM's automation is live on that character's account.

## Description
Write ONE description of what the reel is about, plus hashtags. The same text goes in the description box on every platform (Instagram, YouTube, X). It is doing real SEO work: narrate what happens, include the full recipe (ingredients, amounts, steps), never leave it thin. The detailed description is what took a reel from about 35K to 95K views.
Closing line differs by platform (see `pipeline/funnel.md`). Instagram and Facebook: one of the fixed comment keywords (GET GUIDE, GET RECIPE, YES) plus the follow ask, because LinkDM works there. YouTube, TikTok and X cannot take a DM: close with "link is in the description" and put the link in the description (and a pinned comment where allowed). Never invent a keyword. Do not say "free".
Save it as `caption.txt` beside the final video.

## Captions on the video (check, do not redo)
Two layers: the Remotion motion-graphics captions are the main readable ones; a second tiny caption track is burned into a corner (CapCut or equivalent), small enough not to compete. If the final has only one layer, send it back: Stage = Needs fixes, "second caption layer missing".

## Posting
1. Publer: Instagram, plus YouTube and X where the character has them. Facebook is NOT a separate destination in Publer.
2. When the Instagram post is live, use Instagram's own Share button to push it to the character's Facebook page, so views and engagement stay on the one post.
3. Never schedule for the same hour. A video finished today goes out tomorrow, or at least a few hours out.
4. Row: Stage = Scheduled with the date; once live, Stage = Posted and the Posted date.

## Recipe
Add or update the recipe in the Notion recipe library from the plan's recipe card and the end-result still. Steps must actually work; resolve the plan's "gaps to confirm" with Dave first. The plan doc's recipe card stays the master copy for the later move to a website.

## Dave AI / course promo posts
Same publishing rule: the description is filled in on every platform. Different CTA: a Notion card offering a free skill download; the commenter clicks the button, downloads the skill from the repo, and that step also gives course access.
