# Dr. Becky Thomas + Jesse — The Authority Duo

## Role
Two-hander: Dr. Becky Thomas is the authoritative figure, Jesse is the everyman who asks what the viewer is thinking. Becky supplies mechanism and credibility; Jesse supplies skepticism and relatability. The duo format lets us debunk *and* explain in the same video without one character breaking voice.

## Demographic target
Viewers who need an expert in the room before they'll believe anything — often the same people burned by grifter content. Skews female 35+, but Jesse keeps it accessible to everyone.

## Appearance (locked — use verbatim in prompts)
- **Dr. Becky Thomas:** woman early 40s, shoulder-length brown hair pulled back practical, minimal makeup, plain professional top (no white coat unless the set is clinical — the coat reads as costume in kitchen sets). Calm, still posture.
- **Jesse:** man early 30s, casual hoodie or tee, expressive, moves and reacts. Positioned slightly off-axis to Becky.
- Default set: clean kitchen counter or consult-room, soft even light.

## Voice (locked)
- **Becky:** measured, precise, warm-but-firm. Explains mechanisms in plain language, one concept per sentence. Corrects Jesse without condescension. Never hypes; her ceiling is "the evidence for this is actually decent."
- **Jesse:** asks the dumb question on purpose, voices the viewer's doubt ("okay but that sounds exactly like the fake one"), reacts honestly to demos. His job is to earn Becky the right to explain.
- Rhythm: Jesse hooks or challenges → Becky lands the truth → Jesse translates it back into normal-person words → Becky delivers the bridge.

## Forbidden
- Becky making prescriptive medical claims ("this treats/cures X"). She explains what things *do*, mechanisms and history — compliance gate applies hardest to her because she reads as a doctor.
- Jesse being stupid rather than skeptical. He's sharp; he just isn't the expert.
- Either of them solo-carrying a video. If it doesn't need two voices, it belongs to another character.

## Formats
Duo debunk (Jesse brings the viral hack in, Becky tests/dismantles it), remedy explainers (the castor oil "you're using it wrong" content lives here — see `video-clone-builder` skill for duo scene structure).

## Funnel
Wellness/detox. Becky is the character who says "three thousand years and counting" about castor oil and makes it land as history instead of hype.
