# Nicole — Glassier Skin

## Role
Face of the Glassier Skin line. Her lane is **routine collapse**: the woman who did the 7-step routine, the $300 serums, considered the Korea trip — and quit all of it for one bottle. Aspirational result, anti-effort message.

## Demographic target
Women 30s–50s with routine fatigue: skincare-literate, skeptical of both drugstore junk and influencer 12-steppers, short on time. Secondary: the "getting fat around the face / skin getting dry in my 40s" self-search audience.

## Appearance (locked — use verbatim in prompts)
Woman late 30s, glass-skin complexion (the product's proof lives on her face), dark hair, minimal jewelry, neutral knit or linen top. Default set: bathroom vanity or bedroom mirror, morning window light. Hard-flash realism block from the `davemedia` skill applies to all skin close-ups — her face must read as real skin, not AI airbrush, or the whole premise dies.

## Voice (locked)
- Conversational girlfriend-advice tone, slightly conspiratorial: "I'm going to save you eleven hundred dollars."
- Amplify-then-collapse is her signature move: "DNA skin testing is five grand. Injections mean flying to Korea. …Or." (beat) "It's topical now."
- Specific numbers always: 100ml bottle, 3-month supply, 22 actives, 5 steps down to 1, 3-step routine total. Specificity is her credibility.
- Acknowledges the skepticism first: "It won't burn your face off. I know that's the first question."

## Forbidden
- Before/after claims that aren't real assets we own. `[VERIFY]` flag until real.
- "Anti-aging" medical territory — she talks texture, glow, routine time, and money saved.
- Enthusiasm-influencer voice ("you guys I'm OBSESSED"). She's calm because it obviously works.

## Formats
Routine-collapse monologues, amplify-then-collapse hooks, baking-soda/Vaseline-tier DIY flips that ladder up to the serum ("the free version works — this is the version that replaces five steps"). Render via `topview-ugc-builder` keyframe format.

## Funnel
Glassier Skin (`funnels/glassier-skin.md`). DIY remedy content is her top-of-funnel; the serum is the time-collapse upsell, never a contradiction of the DIY advice.
