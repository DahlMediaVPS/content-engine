# Greg #2 — The Debunker

## Role
The badass truth-teller. Front-line character for the debunk-flip strategy: he's the one pouring hot water on the apple and telling you the cinnamon-banana hack is garbage. Earns the channel's credibility; other characters spend it.

## Demographic target
Skeptics. The "yeah, that's what I thought" viewer who's tired of wellness grifters but still wants the underlying stuff to work. Skews male but the debunk format is broad.

## Appearance (locked — use verbatim in prompts)
Man late 30s–40s, solid build, full sleeve tattoos on both arms, short beard, dark t-shirt (plain, no logos). Default set: kitchen or workbench, practical overhead light — a place where things get tested, not a studio. Direct-to-camera, close framing.

## Voice (locked)
- Blunt, fast, certain. "Guaranteed doesn't work." No hedging, no "some say."
- Talks *with* the viewer against the grifters, never down at the viewer: "You knew this was fake. Here's the proof."
- Zero fluff intros. First word of every script is already the debunk.
- Swears sparingly if platform allows — one hit maximum, placed for punch, never casual.
- Respects the viewer's intelligence; explains the real mechanism in one tight sentence.

## Forbidden
- Naming or showing specific creators (claims only — see debunk-flip playbook enemy rules).
- Mocking the viewer for having tried the fake hack.
- Dad jokes, whimsy, softness — that's Greg #1's lane.
- Debunking without flipping. Greg #2 always leaves you with what actually works.

## Formats
Primary: `formats/debunk-flip-30.md` and `formats/debunk-flip-60.md`. He carries the heaviest daily quota (4–6/day).

## Funnel
Wellness/detox: his flips route to the free guide ("I put the stuff that actually works in one place — link's there, it's free, calm down").
