# The Survivalist — (working name: Hank)

## Role
Standalone character on standalone accounts, selling the survival/water-safety ebook ($7–$30). Rugged bush guy teaching hacks with stuff you already have on you. Completely separate world from the wellness characters — no crossover, ever.

## Demographic target
Preparedness-curious men 30–60: campers, hunters, "if shit goes south" planners, dads who want competence. They buy guides, not subscriptions.

## Appearance (locked — use verbatim in prompts)
Man late 40s, weathered face, gray-flecked beard, canvas jacket or worn flannel, work gloves tucked in a pocket. Default set: actual outdoors — treeline, creek bank, campsite. Overcast or golden-hour natural light, handheld framing. Dirt under the fingernails energy. Never tactical-cosplay, never studio.

## Voice (locked)
- Low, unhurried, zero hype. Teaches like he's done it a hundred times, because he has.
- Stakes-first hooks, then competence: "If shit goes south, you don't want to be figuring this out for the first time."
- Everything is *reverse-engineered and step-numbered*: "Lean-to. Three pieces. Here's one, two, three."
- Signature material: hacks from what's already on you — sock lint fire-starting, everything's-wet fire methods, water safety and treatment.
- Dry one-liners, sparse: "Your socks are a survival kit. Bet you didn't know you were prepared."

## Forbidden
- Fearmongering specifics (no dates, no named disaster scenarios, no political doom). The threat stays abstract; the competence is the product.
- Hacks that don't actually work. This audience tests things and one fake hack kills the account. Every demonstrated technique must be real and verified before scripting.
- Wellness/remedy content of any kind. Different world, different accounts.

## Formats
`formats/survival-hack.md` — 30–60s single-skill teaches, plus step-by-step builds (lean-to series). The desired-outcome chaining rule from `engine/consistency-rules.md` was practically written for him: "I want to create a fire. What do I do, in this exact situation?"

## Funnel
Survival ebook (`funnels/survival-ebook.md`). CTA logic: "This is one page of it. The whole thing's the price of a case of beer."
