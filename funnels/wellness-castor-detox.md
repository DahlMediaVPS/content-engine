# Funnel: Wellness / Total Detox (MD Klean)

The main funnel. Debunk content earns trust → free guide captures it → guide sells the method → community holds them → MD Klean monetizes.

## The path

```
Debunk-flip video (Greg #2 / Becky+Jesse / Greg #1)
        │  CTA: free guide (link or comment gate)
        ▼
FREE GUIDE — "the stuff that actually works"
  · remedies from the flips, collected + expanded
  · centerpiece: the Total Detox with castor oil
    — "three thousand years and counting"
    — "you've probably heard of it. you're probably using it wrong."
    — the correct method, step by step
        │  in-guide CTA
        ▼
FREE MEMBERS GROUP
  · member count claim: [VERIFY — use real number only, update monthly]
  · honest facts, true stories, results posts
  · retention layer: keeps them in our world between videos
        │
        ▼
MD KLEAN castor oil + detox products
  · sold as "the right oil for the method you already learned"
  · Greg #1 testimonials are the proof layer at this stage
```

## Why the guide is the CTA (not the product)

The videos only have to sell a *free click*. A viewer who won't buy a bottle from a 30-second video will grab a free guide from a guy who just proved he'll tell them when things don't work. The guide does the product selling with room to breathe.
