## The positioning logic

- **Amplify:** the alternatives at their true extreme — $5,000 DNA skin testing, injection tourism to Korea/Beverly Hills.
- **Collapse:** "It's topical now." One bottle, one step, three-step routine total.
- **The sell is time and sanity**, not transformation: "If you're sick of the routine and you want your twenty minutes back." Results language stays in texture/glow/simplicity territory.
- DIY lane never gets contradicted by product lane. The serum is for people who *could* do the 5-step DIY but don't want to. "The free version works. This is the version that replaces five steps."

## Audience entry hooks (mirror beats)

- "In my forties and my face is changing" self-searchers
- Routine fatigue: 7-steppers who dread their own bathroom counter
- Skincare-literate skeptics who've been burned by influencer serums (debunk-adjacent: Nicole can borrow Greg #2-style ingredient debunks in her own voice)

## Compliance rails

- No medical/anti-aging claims, no "safe" as an absolute (the "won't burn your face off" line is positioned as texture/gentleness, not a safety guarantee — keep it in that register).
- All numbers on the spec stack (100ml / 3-month / 22 actives / 5→1) verified against the actual product before any script ships.
