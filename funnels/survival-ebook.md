# Funnel: Survival Ebook

The Survivalist's funnel. Fully separate world: own character, own accounts, no crossover with wellness. Shortest funnel of the three — content demonstrates competence, ebook sells it in full, price point is impulse-tier.

## The path

```
Survival hack videos (30–60s, one real skill each)
  · sock-lint fire starting · everything's-wet fire methods
  · lean-to build series · water safety & treatment
        │  CTA: "that's one page of the guide"
        ▼
SURVIVAL / WATER SAFETY EBOOK — $7–$30 [pick price via test, see below]
  · every hack from the videos, plus the full systems
  · water safety, treatment, storage as the anchor section
  · step-by-step builds with diagrams
        │  optional back-end
        ▼
Future: gear checklist upsell / expanded guide bundle
```

## Why this funnel is different

- **No free-guide layer.** The videos *are* the free layer — each one proves a full skill. The ask is small enough ($7–$30) to convert straight from content.
- **Proof structure:** every video ends with the working result on screen. The ebook CTA logic is always "you just watched one page work."
- **Price test:** launch at $9.95, test against $17 and $27 with identical content. The audience buys competence, not length — the winning price is an empirics question, not a positioning one.

## Content pillars → ebook chapters

| Video pillar | Ebook section |
|---|---|
| Fire (lint, wet-weather methods) | Fire: 5 situations, 5 methods |
| Shelter (lean-to series) | Shelter builds, step-numbered |
| Water (safety, treatment) | Water: the anchor chapter — find, treat, store |
| On-you hacks (socks, everyday carry) | "You're already carrying a kit" |

Every ebook chapter should have at least 3 proving videos before we push it as a selling point.

## CTA copy bank

- "That's one page. The whole guide costs less than the tarp you should already own."
- "If shit goes south, you don't want to be figuring this out for the first time."
- "The whole thing's the price of a case of beer."

## Compliance rails

- Every technique in videos AND ebook is real and tested — this audience verifies, and one fake hack ends the account.
- Water treatment content: prevention and treatment of *water*, never medical treatment of people.
- No doom-dating, no named-disaster fearmongering. Abstract stakes, concrete skills.
